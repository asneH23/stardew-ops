I know some of you might be wondering why the portrait version has earrings while the sprite version doesn't. 💕

This was a purely visual decision. Since the sprite is very small, adding the earrings made the design look a bit too busy and affected its readability. So I decided to leave them out of the sprite to keep it clean and easy to read.

The earrings are still part of the character's design—they just aren't shown in the sprite for visual clarity. I hope that makes sense, and thank you all so much for your understanding and support! 🌸
